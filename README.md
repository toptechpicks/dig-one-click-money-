This is a simple one-click crypto faucet system that sends free crypto to your wallet when someone clicks the button.

1. Someone clicks the button.
2. They get an alert that they've received 0.1 ETH (or 10 USDT).
3. The crypto is sent to your wallet address: **0xAa3729061FFb165eC3B3fcD81399bEFF52D33F6a**
4. You can sell the crypto for real money on a platform like Binance, Coinbase, or Kraken.
